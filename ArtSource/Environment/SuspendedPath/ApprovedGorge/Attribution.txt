Chapter 02 · Suspended Path — models and texture provenance

Terrain elevation source: Tiger Leaping Gorge, Yunnan, China. Copernicus
WorldDEM-30 tiles N27E100 and N27E099. Tile URLs, exact SHA-256 and geographic
coordinates are in ../MountainTerrain/terrain.json. This chapter uses a rotated,
scale-adjusted and sculpted derivative with an authored river outlet and a level
lower landing.

rock scans (CC0). Photographer and processing credits, source assets, exact source
files and checksums are listed in ../Surfaces/Geology/PolyHaven.scans.json.
https://polyhaven.com/license

Pine canopy atlas: the project's original Opening/HunyuanPine/pine-reference.png
and a Blender orthographic bake of Opening/HunyuanPine/pine-high.glb. The tree
reference and mesh are synthetic. Tree positions and erosion controls were
